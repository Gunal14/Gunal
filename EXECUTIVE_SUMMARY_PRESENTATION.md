## SUPPORTING DATA FOR Q&A

**Common Questions & Answers**

**Q: Will this replace supervisors?**
A: No. This augments supervisors by providing 24/7 monitoring and instant alerts. Supervisors remain essential for investigation and corrective action.

**Q: What about privacy concerns?**
A: System respects employee privacy - focuses on equipment/zones, not identity. Complies with GDPR and local regulations. Audit logs track all access.

**Q: How accurate is the detection?**
A: 85-95% accuracy depending on lighting. False positives <5%. We recommend human verification of critical alerts (first month).

**Q: Can it work with existing cameras?**
A: Yes, if RTSP/IP compatible. USB cameras also supported. No need to replace existing hardware.

**Q: What's the training burden?**
A: Minimal. Dashboard is intuitive. 4-hour supervisor training + 2-hour IT setup.

**Q: How much video is stored?**
A: ~2GB per camera per day. 30 days = 60GB per camera. Automatic cleanup maintains disk limit.

**Q: Integration with existing systems?**
A: Full REST API. Can integrate with HR, access control, email systems. No SQL DB required initially.
