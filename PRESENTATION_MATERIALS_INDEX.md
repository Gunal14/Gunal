## HOW TO CONVERT TO POWERPOINT

### Step 1: Create Skeleton (30 minutes)
1. Open PowerPoint
2. Create 40 blank slides
3. Add title slide with your company branding
4. Apply consistent color scheme throughout

### Step 2: Add Content (1-2 hours)
1. **Slides 1-10:** Copy content from EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_PRESENTATION.md
   - Paste text sections
   - Format with bullet points
   - Add your company colors/fonts

2. **Slides 11-25:** Create graphics from ARCHITECTURE_DIAGRAMS.md
   - Sketch or use Draw.io for diagrams
   - Add 1-2 diagrams per slide
   - Use flowchart shapes for processes

3. **Slides 26-40:** Add business case and closing
   - Include ROI tables
   - Add implementation timeline
   - Create action items summary

### Step 3: Visual Polish (1 hour)
- Consistent fonts (2 max)
- Color scheme (3-4 colors)
- Icons for emphasis
- Charts for metrics
- Images/screenshots where helpful

### Step 4: Review & Practice (30 minutes)
- Check for consistency
- Verify all facts and figures
- Practice talking points
- Time your presentation

**Total time:** 3-4 hours for professional presentation

## PRESENTATION DO'S AND DON'Ts

**DO:**
✅ Lead with ROI (most executives care about money)
✅ Use visuals for complex concepts
✅ Tell stories (use case examples)
✅ Show real data (performance metrics)
✅ Practice beforehand
✅ Have backup slides for technical deep-dives

**DON'T:**
❌ Get too technical too quickly
❌ Show all 16 architectural layers to non-technical audience
❌ Use small fonts (audience sits far away)
❌ Read slides verbatim
❌ Skip the ROI slide
❌ Go over 40 minutes without break
